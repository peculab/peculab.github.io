READING THE EVIDENCE

What this map can tell us.

This is an affiliation network: each node is a shared attribute, and each line means some connections carry both attributes. It does not show relationships between those people, relationship closeness, activity, or commercial value. Organization names do not imply partnership or endorsement.

  • Companies must appear at least 3 times. Edges require at least 4 shared records and positive pointwise mutual information (PMI). Only nodes with retained edges appear.
  • Edge weight = shared-record count × log₂(shared-record count × 1,072 ÷ the product of both attribute counts). Weighted strength sums incident edge weights.
  • Betweenness counts weighted shortest paths through an attribute, using inverse edge weight as distance. Values are unnormalized and describe the supplied, pruned graph—not Pecu's personal influence.
  • Role levels, themes, and organization countries come from the supplied enriched CSV and may contain classification errors. 883 records lack a reliable country inference. Themes overlap; percentages must not be added together.
  • Original organization labels are preserved: “Google” and “谷歌” remain separate nodes. This snapshot is not a live LinkedIn feed. Positions are for readability, not geography.

Source: the supplied LinkedIn Connections Enriched CSV and Affiliation Network GraphML. This public page includes aggregate counts only; no contact names, emails, or profile links are published.
