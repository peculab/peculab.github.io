<p>Data Science &amp; AI Agents Design</p>

<p>The generative AI wave makes it important to define a spectrum of AI roles. Every part matters, from computing suppliers to the end users of AI services.</p>

<p>With Computex 2024 focusing on hardware, we must recognize that future AI value will depend not only on hardware, but also on collecting, managing, and using data—moving from data for model training toward the design of AI agents.</p>

<p>The AI role spectrum and definitions that came to me in a dream.</p>

<p>From the NTU CS+X course series to the courses I now teach at NTNU, I have observed that students from traditional electrical engineering and computer science backgrounds often find career paths in areas 1 and 2: AI computing suppliers and AI platform developers. Students from other disciplines may be better suited to areas 3 and 4: AI agent designers and AI application developers.</p>

<p>Roles 1–4 all require programming. Only role 5 can be pursued without knowing a programming language.</p>

<p>1. AI computing suppliers: provide the computing capacity and hardware support that form the foundation of AI systems.</p>

<p>2. AI framework developers: platforms such as Hugging Face and LangChain provide tools and environments for building and deploying advanced models.</p>

<p>3. AI agent designers: create and improve model interfaces and interactions so human–computer communication feels natural. This includes applying psychology to technical design and using advanced frameworks to train semantic understanding models, connecting technology with practical applications.</p>

<p>4. AI application developers: use frameworks and tools to create industry-specific solutions, address business problems, and ensure that solutions can operate effectively in commercial environments.</p>

<p>5. Users of packaged AI services: end users who use no-code or low-code platforms to improve workflows and decisions.</p>

<p>Recently I have felt God's guidance more clearly each night, showing me how to cultivate AI agent designers able to work across diverse application contexts.</p>

<p>This is a change in thinking as well as technology: a move from excessive dependence on hardware toward practical software applications.</p>

<p>I hope to develop a comprehensive, concrete strategy and find people who want to build a complementary, efficient ecosystem together. Computing supply, platform development, and user-interface innovation are closely connected and can advance the industry as a whole.</p>

<p>Past and present PecuLab students have successfully applied interdisciplinary knowledge to real problems. In particular, students without electrical engineering or computer science backgrounds have combined AI with their own fields to innovate and gain new abilities. This combination has advanced work within their disciplines and brought fresh perspectives and solutions to the wider industry.</p>
