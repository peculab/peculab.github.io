<p>I know I am not the most outstanding teacher. I was asked to take responsibility for this work because I am willing to pursue its goal pragmatically. Rather than dwell on whether every resource is in place, I simply hope to ease classroom teachers' workloads and create a starting point for students who have limited resources but want to learn.</p>

<p>Our goal is to provide equal learning opportunities for families and students with fewer resources. In particular, we hope to offer diverse elective courses in senior high and vocational schools outside major cities without adding to teachers' preparation burden. Teachers do not need extra training or prerecorded lessons; they can focus on supporting their students.</p>

<p>Our design also avoids complicated equipment preparation. Students only need access to YouTube and a Google account to participate, greatly reducing pressure on school IT coordinators. We want every step to be simple so educational resources can reach the students and families who need them with as little friction as possible.</p>

<p>This nationwide live course is one way we put educational equity into practice. We hope every child can access new learning resources, choose a learning path, and perhaps discover an opportunity to work with AI and move toward a future with more possibilities.</p>

<p>To be honest, there are still many challenges. Resources, services, and system optimization all have room for improvement. We know this is not a perfect solution, nor can it compare with more extensively resourced educational services.</p>

<p>What we offer is an “economy class” learning option: a way for more ordinary students to get onto the runway rather than be excluded from educational opportunity.</p>

<p>We do not wait until everything is ready before setting out. We believe that once we start walking, a path can emerge. We have overcome administrative obstacles and established a working system that reduces teachers' burdens and gives schools unable to offer enough electives another choice.</p>

<p>As long as teachers are willing to support their students, our team will take responsibility for lesson preparation, teaching, assignments, and grading, helping students with fewer resources open a door to opportunity.</p>

<p>Every attempt aims to lower barriers to education and give more children the freedom to choose their learning paths. We are doing more than transmitting knowledge: we are building a bridge toward the future for students and families facing difficult circumstances. We believe that by taking steps, we can open wider paths and turn hope into reality.</p>

<p>None of this has been accomplished by one person alone. It depends on collaboration and support: government teams moving things forward, administrators coordinating, and schoolteachers contributing. Each part matters. Small acts of goodwill have accumulated into what exists today.</p>

<p>Our contribution is only one part of that effort: connecting resources to create another learning option for schools and students with limited access. No one is doing this single-handedly. Countless people's work has made it possible. From administrative processes to course design and delivery, a belief in educational equity holds the effort together.</p>

<p>I believe that conviction comes from our hopes for the future. Everyone who participates contributes in their own way. Many challenges remain, but if that goodwill keeps accumulating, small streams can become a river, creating lasting change and more opportunities for children to choose their futures.</p>

<p>I chose this path not because I had a perfect answer, but because I believe we find suitable approaches by taking action. Mistakes are not frightening in themselves. What I value most is the room teachers and students have given me to make them. Their patience and support allow me to keep adjusting and improving.</p>

<p>I am grateful for this real, shared journey. It has helped us grow together and taught me that education's meaning lies not in perfection, but in the concrete efforts we make at each step to create more possibilities for one another.</p>
