<p>#BeThePeculiar #台師大科技系</p>

<p>Small models for specific tasks: PecuLab's approach to developing AI talent.</p>

<p>As LLMs grow and the arms race intensifies, we choose to develop people with limited resources rather than join a spending contest.</p>

<p>This time, PecuLab project students fine-tuned a smaller model, yentinglin/Llama-3-Taiwan-8B-Instruct. Thank you to 陳縕儂's doctoral student for the open-source Traditional Chinese model on Hugging Face.</p>

<p>It gives us a foundation for building an AI that helps people with social anxiety practice conversation, and for exploring how retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) can improve multi-turn dialogue.</p>

<p>Environment and training strategy: computing was provided by Ubitus. Thank you, Wesley Kuo.</p>

<p>Hardware: two NVIDIA A5000 GPUs, each with 24GB VRAM.</p>

<p>Model: Llama3 8B—small enough, but useful.</p>

<p>Techniques: 4-bit quantization and QLoRA to reduce memory requirements.</p>

<p>Optimization: use RAG to retrieve context and improve conversational continuity.</p>

<p>Students collected data from communities including Dcard, PTT, and Reddit and organized 428 annotated conversations to help the AI continue dialogue naturally instead of seeming to start again each time.</p>

<p>What were the results?</p>

<p>Original model: conversations easily broke off, and each response felt like restarting the AI.</p>

<p>With RAG: the model could refer back to earlier exchanges, making conversation more coherent.</p>

<p>Prompt adjustments: responses became more natural and less academic.</p>

<p>What did we learn?</p>

<p>1. Value is not limited to large models. A small model plus a good strategy can offer a better user experience.</p>

<p>2. RAG is important to multi-turn conversation in this experiment; without that context, the LLM felt as though the conversation was being reset without memory.</p>

<p>3. Limited resources can develop essential skills. Having students train AI themselves offers learning opportunities beyond calling an API.</p>

<p>This experiment showed us that, with a defined use case, we do not necessarily need hundreds of GPUs. A use-case-specific AI can be trained with a single-digit number of A100 GPUs.</p>

<p>If a bicycle gets you somewhere faster, why insist on driving?</p>

<p>Large and small models can cooperate rather than oppose one another, just as different modes of transport coexist. Giant bicycles do not disappear because Tesla exists; more cycling enthusiasts can make them more popular.</p>

<p>PecuLab does not have vast resources to spend on hardware. But give me enough food and sleep, and I can design learning environments where students gain practical abilities and understand the value of technology by working with it.</p>

<p>Investing in and developing people is PecuLab's alternative route through the hardware arms race.</p>

<p>If you want to understand something, do it and modify the source code directly, rather than rely only on information passed through people who have not implemented it themselves.</p>

<p>PecuLab: a virtual base for using AI to train people, not only training AI.</p>

<p>The project is ongoing. I hope we can show it publicly at the year-end exhibition for people to try, helping those who struggle with social interaction.</p>
