<p>#臺師大教育大數據微學程 #電機系 #科技系 #公領系 #PecuLab</p>

<p>Today was full of surprises and pride!</p>

<p>Led by director 林政宏, the NTNU Educational Big Data microprogram team went to the Kaohsiung Exhibition Center for the Ministry of Education's academic year 113 digital content development midterm exchange. We presented the second phase's substantial results. There were too many projects to fit on the posters, so visitors used QR codes to explore them.</p>

<p>Students Irene Chang (張乃云), 潘允蕙, and Walli Hu (胡亞莉), who also served as teaching assistants, joined us. Through posters and demo videos, they presented final projects from Educational Big Data Project Development and Educational Data Mining Project Development.</p>

<p>These projects used generative AI models available on Hugging Face, demonstrating the possibilities of AI in education.</p>

<p>The projects were full of creativity and technical skill: an AI Korean tutor, exploring music through Fourier transforms, relationship graphs, a learning portfolio converter, programmatically generated maps, CRUD operations in LINE, a lesson-plan generator, a poetry helper, and a PDF chat assistant. Each application showed students' inventive thinking and technical ability.</p>

<p>I was especially delighted to see students introducing the projects spontaneously in fluent English. They demonstrated not only technical skills but also the ability to communicate internationally, an important strength for their future development.</p>

<p>These projects relied on combining data science with AI agent design and implementation. Creating and improving model interfaces and interactions to make human–computer communication natural includes applying psychological principles to technical design and using advanced AI frameworks to train semantic understanding models, connecting technology with practical applications.</p>

<p>The exchange showed our team's work and gave me a deeper appreciation of students' potential and ability in digital content development. It strengthened my belief that they will bring new insights and possibilities to educational technology.</p>

<p>I am proud of every participating student, teacher, and administrative colleague, and I look forward to more moments like this.</p>

<p>Thank you to every colleague and student who participated and supported us. Your collective efforts made this possible!</p>
