<p>Today I took my child to a very special Story Time.</p>

<p>It was not at a library, but at Kelsey Creek Farm Park in Bellevue. The children listened to stories beside the farm. Afterward, they could take an activity sheet to find animals, read the signs, and observe at their own pace.</p>

<p>I love this connection between public resources: reading steps outside the library, and children begin learning in the real world.</p>
