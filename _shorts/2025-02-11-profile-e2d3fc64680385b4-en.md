<p>Python AI Hands-on Introduction 🚀 Start from scratch and build AI applications!</p>

<p>Beginning with everyday issues, this course introduces practical AI applications through Python. No programming experience is required. If you can open a browser and use Google Colab and YouTube, you can get started!</p>

<p>Course features</p>

<p>🔹 Beginner-friendly, but you must be willing to try things and look for solutions when you encounter bugs, rather than passively wait for an answer.<br>
🔹 Practical work makes AI useful and engaging, from chatbots and data analysis to image recognition.<br>
🔹 Learn computational thinking and problem-solving rather than memorizing syntax.</p>

<p>Who is it for?</p>

<p>✅ People who have never learned Python but are willing to experiment and debug.<br>
✅ Anyone who wants to build AI applications such as chatbots, data analysis tools, and image-recognition projects.<br>
✅ Learners interested in AI and programming who want to understand their effect on daily life.<br>
❌ If error messages frighten you and you feel you must memorize all the syntax before starting, this course may not suit you. It emphasizes learning by doing and solving problems as they arise!</p>

<p>Course content</p>

<p>Foundations<br>
1️⃣ What is AI? Basic concepts and applications.<br>
2️⃣ Python programming basics: build a simple chatbot.<br>
3️⃣ Data processing and analysis: use AI to support decisions.</p>

<p>Hands-on AI applications<br>
4️⃣ Data visualization: analyze personal habits and spending through charts.<br>
5️⃣ Introductory machine learning: predict house prices with a simple regression model.<br>
6️⃣ Image recognition: train AI to recognize handwritten digits and experience deep learning.<br>
7️⃣ Natural language processing: analyze social discussion trends and movie-review keywords.<br>
8️⃣ Web scraping: collect news data automatically for analysis.</p>

<p>Assignments and assessment</p>

<p>📌 Weekly hands-on assignments requiring Python code submissions.<br>
📌 Automated feedback from the 批改娘 AI grading tool to help you identify mistakes promptly.<br>
📌 An independent final AI application project to demonstrate what you have learned.</p>

<p>What you will gain</p>

<p>✅ Practical Python and AI experience.<br>
✅ Problem-solving ability beyond Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V.<br>
✅ Confidence in debugging and learning from mistakes as you build AI applications.</p>

<p>This course teaches a way of thinking, not just syntax. If you are willing to try and not afraid of mistakes, join us! 🚀</p>
