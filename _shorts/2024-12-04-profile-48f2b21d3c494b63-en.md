<p>A year ago, while discussing Jubo AI's development strategy with Shih-Chung Jessy Kang, we explored market trends and Jubo's distinctive position together.</p>

<p>I suggested that instead of following the trend toward large-scale fine-tuning, we could use an LLM for intention detection, combine it with fuzzy search to match users' data accurately, and make stronger data governance the core of a practical approach.</p>

<p>I am grateful that Jessy trusted me and accepted a less conventional voice. That recognition was hard to describe, and it strengthened my determination to lead the team in testing an idea that had not yet been widely discussed.</p>

<p>The direction helped Jubo find a distinctive value proposition and a path toward sustainable development.</p>

<p>I especially want to thank Jessy and the colleagues who built Jubo's strong data foundation. Its substantial base of real users gave us an opportunity to innovate in care through data.</p>

<p>That foundation gave us room to explore and a solid basis for each development step.</p>

<p>Reviewing our work with Jubo's CTO today, we recognized a new software-development approach: from describing scenarios to constructing semantics, and from using an LLM to interact with databases to searching and connecting data. Every step involved technical challenges and insight into the use case, demonstrating creativity, technology, and close teamwork.</p>

<p>As the end of the year approaches, we have reached a milestone worth remembering.</p>

<p>N-Copilot will appear at tomorrow's medical exhibition at National Cheng Kung University's booth. The journey from the first idea to a working implementation has been full of challenges and growth.</p>

<p>These results depend on the joint efforts of Jubo's AI team, PecuLab members, and Minerva project students.</p>

<p>Our collective ideas and work have gradually made the concept real. Every partner's expertise and creativity were essential. I feel fortunate to work with them.</p>

<p>Our Jubo Gateway deserves particular mention. It integrates LLM and MongoDB capabilities to search across collections and generate reports through natural language. This gives users a more convenient, capable query tool and opens further application possibilities.</p>

<p>We know this is only the beginning. I hope we can continue working together to bring practical solutions and innovative value to more people.</p>
