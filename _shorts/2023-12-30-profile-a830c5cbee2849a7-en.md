<p>A new course for the second semester of academic year 112 (2023–2024): Research on Web-Assisted Learning Systems.</p>

<p>I want students to go beyond basic AI tools with graphical interfaces. By programming their own customized learning support systems, they can develop a deeper understanding of AI applications and a broader range of practical technological abilities.</p>

<p>In this course, students will learn to develop a generative AI learning support system using the ChatGPT API, Python LangChain, and APIs available through Hugging Face. The goal is to help them understand the basic principles of generative AI and apply them to innovative, useful educational applications.</p>

<p>The course will cover:</p>

<p>1. Fundamentals of generative AI, including how it works and where it can be applied.<br>
2. Interacting with language models and developing applications using the ChatGPT API.<br>
3. Using Python LangChain for data processing and AI model integration.<br>
4. Exploring Hugging Face and using its resources and tools to enhance AI applications.<br>
5. Case analysis and hands-on practice so students can design and implement their own learning support systems.</p>

<p>Through the course, students will gain a deeper understanding of generative AI's capabilities and learn to apply the technology to educational tools that support teaching and learning.</p>

<p>Undergraduate juniors and above, master's students, and doctoral students who want to experience working with AI through programming are welcome to learn together!</p>
