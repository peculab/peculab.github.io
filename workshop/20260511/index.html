From market data to an explainable Agentic AI decision.

A focused 15-minute talk led into a complete coding example. Students could follow how market data becomes a visual pattern, an agent action, a risk review, and finally a human-readable recommendation.

The learning path

One pipeline, with every decision point visible.

01

Represent

Convert OHLC windows into CULP + GAF images that a CNN can inspect.

02

Recognize

Use a CNN pattern probe to turn visual structure into model evidence.

03

Act

Let the DQN/DRL policy propose an action from the current state.

04

Review

Approve, reduce, or block the proposal, then explain the final advice.
