來自孩子真實學習現場的文章札記。

Short essays and observations from Seattle schools, libraries, homes, community programs, youth projects, and AI learning experiments. The goal is not to rank education systems, but to notice where each system places the real cost of learning: institutions, families, communities, or children themselves.
