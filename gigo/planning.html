一班幾個人，
才能好好教，也做得下去？A class that works
for learners and teachers.

建議先以 10 人、1 位主教＋1 位助教、6 堂 US$180 提案。8 人是下列分潤假設下的損益兩平人數，不是有充裕餘額的招生目標。Propose 10 learners, one lead plus one assistant, and US$180 for six sessions. Eight learners is the break-even threshold under the assumptions below, not a comfortable enrollment target.

10 人最適合先試Start with 10

五組搭檔、五套操作教具，再備一套替換。主教負責全班引導，助教協助操作、轉場與輪流。Five pairs and five working kits, plus a spare. The lead guides the group; the assistant supports hands-on work, transitions and turn-taking.

8 人可開，但餘額很薄Eight is a thin margin

以 US$30／堂、30% 分潤與本頁成本估算，8 人每堂僅剩 US$4，10 人剩 US$40。At US$30 per session, a 30% city share and the modeled costs, eight learners leave US$4 per class; ten leave US$40.

先談合作開課Discuss a city partnership

公開多功能室租價不利單班試辦。優先洽談課程承包／分潤與可用教室；不能預設已有優惠。The public multipurpose-room rental is costly for a single pilot. Discuss a course contract, revenue share and a suitable classroom; discounted space is not assumed.

02 / VENUE MAKES THE DIFFERENCE02 / VENUE MAKES THE DIFFERENCE

同樣 10 人，合作與租場差很多。Same ten learners. Very different venue costs.

方案 A：課程合作／分潤A / City course partnership

試算先假設市府收取學費 30%，包含場地與報名服務，不再另加公開場租。這是規劃假設，尚未查到本課程可適用的實際比例。The model assumes a 30% city share covering the room and registration, with no additional public room rental. This is a planning assumption; no applicable contract percentage has been confirmed.

10 人：每堂收入 $300 − 分潤 $90 − 變動成本 $30 − 固定成本 $140＝餘額 $40；6 堂 $240。Ten learners: $300 revenue − $90 city share − $30 variable costs − $140 fixed costs = $40 per session, or $240 over six sessions.

方案 B：按公開價自行租場B / Rent at published public rates

2026-05-26 租借指南第 4 頁：NKCC 多功能室居民 $125／小時、非居民 $150／小時；週一至四至少租 2 小時。因此每週最低場租為 $250／$300，不是只付 50 分鐘。第 6 頁另列 $300 可退押金。The May 26, 2026 rental guide, page 4, lists NKCC’s multipurpose room at $125/hour resident or $150/hour nonresident, with a two-hour Monday–Thursday minimum. Minimum weekly rent is therefore $250/$300, not 50 minutes. Page 6 also lists a $300 refundable deposit.

採居民場租與 3% 金流假設，10 人每堂約虧 $129；需 15 人才損益兩平，已超過試辦建議規模。應另談合作教室與費率，不以擴班解決。With resident rent and an assumed 3% payment fee, ten learners lose about $129 per session; break-even is 15 learners, above the pilot’s recommended size. Negotiate an appropriate room and arrangement instead of enlarging the class.

03 / TRY YOUR NUMBERS03 / TRY YOUR NUMBERS

把成本填進來，看看一班的餘額。Put the assumptions to work.

教師費包含實際投入的備課、上課與收拾時間。預設主教 $80＝2 小時 × $40；助教 $30＝1 小時 × $30；行政與雜支 $10；設備 $1,000 分 50 次使用攤提＝每堂 $20；耗材每生每堂 $3。全部是可修改的預算值，不是場館或教師報價。Staff pay includes the time actually worked. Defaults: lead $80 = 2 hours × $40; assistant $30 = 1 hour × $30; overhead $10; equipment $1,000 allocated across 50 uses = $20 per session; consumables $3 per learner per session. All are editable budget assumptions, not quotes.

04 / A PILOT WE CAN LEARN FROM04 / A PILOT WE CAN LEARN FROM

招生、設備與一年安排。Enrollment, equipment and the year ahead.

人數與教學配置Group size & staffing

1 位老師單獨帶班，建議先限 6–8 人；10–12 人安排助教。試辦目標 10 人，上限先設 12 人。這是教學配置建議，不是法定師生比。若多數為 K 或需要較多協助，減少名額並重算成本。For a solo instructor, start with 6–8 learners; use an assistant for 10–12. Target 10 and cap the pilot at 12. These are teaching recommendations, not statutory ratios. Reduce enrollment and recalculate costs if many learners need more support.

設備投入與現金Equipment & cash needs

原廠 10 套組頁面列 US$999.95，查詢時顯示缺貨待補。10 人只需 5 套操作＋至少 1 套備用；先確認現有教具，再比較單套、借用或整組採購。設備成本不能在第一梯的營收中忽略。The manufacturer lists the ten-pack at US$999.95 and showed it as backordered when checked. Ten learners need five active kits plus at least one spare. Check existing inventory before comparing individual kits, loans and a bundle. Initial equipment cash must be budgeted.

全年與續班判斷Year plan & continuation

6 梯 × 6 堂＝36 堂；以 US$180 固定價計，30 堂學年 $900、暑期 $180、全年 $1,080／生，均非承諾售價。試辦後檢查出席、每人操作機會、除錯表達、續報意願與實際工時，再決定是否續開。Six blocks × six sessions = 36. At a constant proposed US$180/block: school year $900, optional summer $180, full year $1,080 per learner. These are planning totals, not committed prices. Review attendance, hands-on turns, debugging explanations, return interest and actual staff time before continuing.

以預設分潤模型、每梯均 10 人且全年 36 堂計：營收 $10,800，扣除分潤、師資、耗材、行政與 $720 設備攤提後，營運餘額 $1,440；改按首年整筆 $1,000 設備現金支出計，現金餘額約 $1,160。這說明單班較適合作為驗證課程與招生需求的試辦；若期待更高盈餘，應提高實際續班率、改善合作條件或另開班，而非無償吸收備課工時。With the default partnership model, ten paid learners per block and 36 sessions: revenue is $10,800 and the operating balance is $1,440 after staff, supplies, overhead, city share and $720 of equipment allocation. Replacing allocation with a full $1,000 first-year equipment purchase gives about $1,160 in cash balance. A single class is mainly a pilot for curriculum and demand. Larger earnings require stronger retention, better terms or another class, with preparation time still paid.

05 / NEXT CONVERSATION WITH NKCC05 / NEXT CONVERSATION WITH NKCC

帶著具體提案，洽談開班條件。Bring a concrete proposal to NKCC.

  • 合作方式：能否以市府課程承包方式辦理？分潤基礎、比例、場地、報名與退款成本各由誰負擔？Partnership: is a contracted city course possible? Confirm revenue-share basis, percentage and responsibility for room, registration and refunds.
  • 時段與空間：週三下午 50 分鐘，另留布置、接送與收拾時間；需五張雙人工作桌、測試空間與一套備用教具。Space and timing: 50 minutes on Wednesday afternoons, plus setup, pickup and cleanup; five paired work areas, testing space and a spare kit.
  • 招生設定：提案 8 人起班、10 人目標、12 人上限；確認居民／非居民定價、適用費用、取消與補課規則。Enrollment: propose minimum 8, target 10, maximum 12; confirm resident/nonresident rates, applicable fees and cancellation/makeup terms.
  • 人員安排：確認實際主教／助教、資格與中心要求；助教若需更多工時，先調整預算。Staff: confirm the lead, assistant, qualifications and center requirements; increase the budget if the assistant needs more paid time.
  • 日期與授課語言：核對學校週三放學及交通時間、假期、中文支援與家長需求後，才公布確切開課日期。Dates and language: verify Wednesday school dismissal, travel time, breaks, Mandarin support and family needs before publishing start dates.
