Faculty and Staff

As you navigate the world of psychology, the faculty and staff become invaluable sources of knowledge, guidance, and support. In addition to teaching, your professors are active researchers, authors, and leading experts in their respective fields. They challenge you in the classroom, but they also open the doors to a wealth of opportunities for research, internships, and hands-on experiences. Meanwhile, the staff—academic advisors, administrative personnel, and student service providers—ensure that your journey is smooth. They guide you through academic regulations, assist with administrative processes, and help you access resources for your academic and personal growth. Engage with them, ask questions, and seek advice. Each interaction, each conversation, can enrich your academic journey, opening new perspectives and possibilities. Your relationship with the faculty and staff goes beyond the bounds of the classroom and can shape your psychology experience in meaningful ways.
