For educators, consultants, founders, and service professionals who want AI to support recurring work.

Seattle AI Project Camp

The planning model is five weekdays, approximately 9:00 a.m.-3:30 p.m., with breaks, small project teams, mentor support, and a family showcase.

Planned program includes

  • Five-day educational program, instruction, and project mentoring
  • Challenge framing, local observation, responsible AI, prototyping, and testing
  • Shared project materials and agreed technology access
  • Prototype, test record, demo, presentation, and reflection
  • Family showcase and individual completion feedback
  • Program administration, planned venue, and required operating insurance

Not included unless announced

  • Lunch, transportation to and from camp, before/after care, personal devices, paid subscriptions, or off-site admission fees

Group boundary: minimum 16 learners to run; maximum 24. Staffing, schedule, and participation terms are confirmed before registration opens.

International partner group · 5-day Seattle core · 16-24 learnersPricing by agreement

Seattle Global AI Innovation Camp

Students work in mixed teams on a Seattle-connected challenge and complete an international AI project in English or an agreed bilingual format.

Base educational package includes

  • Partner planning and participant-readiness survey
  • Two online warm-up sessions before arrival
  • Five-day Seattle educational core and project mentoring
  • Challenge materials, field-research framework, prototyping, and testing
  • English showcase, project feedback, and portfolio evidence
  • One partner debrief and cohort-level outcome summary

Partner is responsible for

  • Recruitment, parental permissions, passports and visas
  • Flights, lodging, meals, travel insurance, and airport transfers
  • Qualified group leaders and supervision outside program hours
  • Medical information, emergency contacts, and timely participant documentation

Group boundary: base planning begins with one cohort of 16 learners; the operating range is 16-24. Learners above the confirmed base, venue, local transportation, special visits, additional staffing, interpretation, and non-program activities are agreed with the partner before delivery.
