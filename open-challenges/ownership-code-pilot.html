THE CHALLENGE

Build trust through useful evidence—not hidden persuasion.

How might a small team reduce the time needed to understand customer questions and prepare useful responses while keeping facts, uncertainty, consent, and human responsibility visible?

WHY IT DESERVES ATTENTION

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

Not “an agent that works.” Evidence another person can inspect.

The first milestone is a reviewable baseline. It should show where the workflow helps, where it fails, what it costs, and when a human must take over.

Relevant

Human reviewers agree that the identified question and pain point fit the scenario.

Source-grounded

Material claims trace to approved evidence; unsupported claims are rejected.

Uncertainty visible

Missing context and limitations are shown instead of silently guessed.

Safe handoff

Risky, unclear, or relationship-sensitive situations reach a person.

Reproducible

Fixtures, model versions, review steps, and expected results are documented.

Cost-aware

Token usage or estimated cost is reported; the core also has a mock path.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Open source does not mean open access to people’s data.

Public project material

  • Source code, prompts, schemas, and evaluation methods
  • Synthetic or approved de-identified examples
  • Public-source links and short attributed summaries
  • Issues, design decisions, and reproducible test results

Never in this repository

  • Customer or prospect lists and contact details
  • Private group posts, messages, or meeting transcripts
  • Credentials, Tokens, or private account configuration
  • Impersonation, covert manipulation, spam, or unapproved outreach
