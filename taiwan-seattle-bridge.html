WHY THIS BRIDGE EXISTS

Talent grows through practice.
Innovation grows through collaboration.

Emerging talent needs opportunities to work beyond the classroom. Companies need a practical way to explore AI with people who understand their challenges. Educators and experienced professionals can help connect the two.

Taiwan–Seattle Bridge brings these contributions together through focused projects, mentor feedback, and shared evidence of what works.

Our first commitment is a small project with a clear outcome. Further study, company adoption, career opportunities, or venture development can follow the evidence and separate agreements.

HOW IT WORKS

A clear brief. A small team. A visible outcome.

  1. 01 / FRAME

    Start with one real question

    A challenge owner defines the context, intended outcome, data boundaries, and a person available for feedback.

  2. 02 / TEAM UP

    Bring complementary strengths

    Match students and professionals with educators, mentors, and regional connectors. Agree on roles and realistic time commitments.

  3. 03 / BUILD & REVIEW

    Make something small and testable

    Research, prototype, and document decisions. Use small tasks and scheduled feedback rather than open-ended commitments.

  4. 04 / SHOW EVIDENCE

    Demonstrate, reflect, decide

    Share the brief, prototype or analysis, validation record, and lessons. Decide together whether another phase is worthwhile.

Before a team starts

A one-page brief · a company feedback contact · a learning and delivery scope · data and publication terms · mentor availability and tools budget · a continue-or-stop decision rule.
