Taiwan × Seattle · Real company challenges

Students in Taiwan and Seattle, working on real challenges.

We are bringing together students, companies, educators, and mentors for projects across Taiwan and Seattle. Companies contribute a business question; students research it, test an idea, and share what they learn. We are looking for collaborators to help shape the first projects.

How it works

Learn by working on a real project

Undergraduate and graduate students work together on a company question, with guidance from educators and mentors. Here is how we expect a project to work.

  1. 01 / FRAME

    A company defines the brief

    Clarify the problem, desired outcome, constraints, available data, and what may be shared publicly. Bring questions that matter and leave room to explore.

  2. 02 / MATCH

    Build an interdisciplinary team

    Match students' disciplines, experience, and availability to the challenge. Faculty link the project to coursework and assessment; campus ambassadors connect students and opportunities.

  3. 03 / MAKE

    Build with mentor checkpoints

    Interview users, research, prototype, and test. Mentors review key decisions and tradeoffs while students document and improve the work.

  4. 04 / PROVE

    Share the results

    Share a useful outcome with the company and prepare a portfolio story: the problem, decisions, prototype, validation, and lessons learned.

What students will share

A project brief, a testable prototype or analysis, a record of feedback and validation, and a presentation the team can deliver. Public portfolio materials respect the confidentiality terms agreed with the company.

We are also exploring an exchange that combines preparation in Taiwan with collaboration in Seattle. Dates, costs, credits, and participating schools are still to be confirmed.
